# Version 2.4.1

- Imported Team Hours now run through one shared current-pay-period totals function
  used by the Dashboard, ensuring local straight time, overtime, total hours, and
  estimated gross pay all include eligible imported shifts.
- After import, the confirmation reports the exact locally calculated straight
  hours, overtime hours, and estimated gross-pay increase, then opens the Dashboard
  so the updated totals are immediately visible.
- Imported shifts outside the configured current pay period remain saved but are
  clearly identified and correctly excluded from current-period payroll totals.
- Private hourly and overtime rates remain local; no teammate pay data is imported.

Android versionCode: 10. No dependency changes from 2.4.0.
