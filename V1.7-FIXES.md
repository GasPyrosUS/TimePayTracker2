# V1.7 Easier Time Entry

Add/Edit Time Entry is now selection-based instead of text-entry based.

- Work Date opens a menu containing the configured 14-day pay period.
- Today's date is marked "Today" when applicable.
- Clock In and Clock Out open standard-time AM/PM menus.
- Time choices are offered in 5-minute increments.
- Exact clock-created times are retained when editing existing entries.
- Unpaid Break is a quick menu for no break, 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
- Existing custom break values remain selectable when editing.
- The preview was simplified and still shows straight time, overtime, paid
  hours, and estimated pay before saving.
- Existing edit/delete functionality remains intact.
- No additional Expo or native dependencies were added.
