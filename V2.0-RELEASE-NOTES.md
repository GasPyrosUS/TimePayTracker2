# Time & Pay Tracker 2.0

- Adds Team Hours with name, date, clock times, straight time, overtime, and total hours only.
- Adds camera/photo timesheet scanning with mandatory editable review and validation before import.
- Keeps private pay calculations in the existing local dashboard and pay-period views.
- Keeps Android, iOS, EAS/APK, dark mode, exports, rollover, saved cards, and GitHub Pages behavior.

## Connection status

The UI, privacy projection, local demo team view, image selection/camera flow, review editor, validation, and reviewed-entry import are implemented. Cross-device team sharing and OCR extraction require the backend endpoints documented in `BACKEND-CONNECTION.md`. No secret is included in the app.
